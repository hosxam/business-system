## Content Gap Analysis

### What's Missing in the Market

**1. Affordable, Structured Exam Prep Materials**
- Current options: expensive courses (CERTA $500+) or free scattered info on YouTube
- **No one is selling affordable digital products like checklists, flashcards, or interview guides at $12-$29**

**2. Step-by-Step Approbation Roadmap (Visual)**
- Most content is video or text-heavy
- No visual flowchart/roadmap product that shows the entire process from start to finish
- **GermanMedPath's free roadmap fills this gap perfectly as lead magnet**

**3. State-by-State Comparison Tools**
- FB groups show heavy demand: "Which state is best for IMGs?"
- Everyone asks about wait times, KP difficulty, job availability by state
- **No product currently consolidates this into an easy-to-digest format**

**4. Fachsprachprüfung (FSP) Specific Prep**
- FSP is the #1 anxiety point for IMGs
- Most prep is either free YouTube or expensive courses
- **Flashcards at $19 targeting FSP questions/vocabulary is priced well**

**5. Interview Preparation for Arzt in Weiterbildung Positions**
- Landing the first job/hospital position is a major pain point
- "How to write a German CV", "How to answer in the interview"
- **The $29 Interview Prep Guide is competitively priced against no direct competitor at that price point**

**6. Arabic-Speaking IMG Market is Underserved**
- Groups like "الأطباء العرب في ألمانيا" have 8.9K members
- No Arabic-language structured prep products exist
- **GermanMedPath could create Arabic-language versions for a niche with less competition**

## 5 High-Value Post Ideas

Based on what IMGs repeatedly ask in Facebook groups and Reddit, these content pieces would generate the highest engagement:

### Post 1: "The #1 Mistake IMGs Make When Applying for Approbation"
**Format:** Carousel post (Facebook/Instagram) or short video (TikTok/Reels)

**Engagement driver:** "Which state are you applying to? Comment below and I'll share what I know."

### Post 2: "Fachsprachprüfung: 10 Most Common Questions They Ask"
**Format:** Carousel or text post
**Hook:** "90% of IMGs fail the Fachsprachprüfung the first time because they don't prepare for THESE specific questions."
**Content:** Share 3-4 example FSP questions with model answers. Show the format of the exam.
**Engagement driver:** "How many of these could you answer? Save this post for later!"
**CTA:** Link to $19 Flashcards (position as FSP prep tool)

### Post 3: "From Zero to Arzt in Weiterbildung: My Exact Timeline"
**Format:** Before/after visual timeline (infographic or video)
**Hook:** "It took me 18 months from B1 German to my first job offer. Here's exactly how."
**Content:** Month-by-month breakdown: B2 course (3 mo), C1 course (3 mo), document submission (2 mo), FSP prep (3 mo), KP prep (3 mo), job search (4 mo). Highlight key milestones and bottlenecks.
**Engagement driver:** "How far along are you in your journey? Pick a stage."
**CTA:** Free roadmap download

### Post 4: "5 Questions You MUST Prepare for Your German Hospital Interview"
**Format:** Short-form video (TikTok/Reels) + carousel
**Hook:** "Your interview for a German hospital position is NOT like any interview you've done before."
**Content:** Share 5 common interview questions:
1. "Warum möchten Sie in Deutschland arbeiten?"
2. "Was wissen Sie über unser Krankenhaus?"
3. "Wie gehen Sie mit Stress um?"
4. "Erzählen Sie uns von Ihrer klinischen Erfahrung"
5. "Wo sehen Sie sich in 5 Jahren?"
Provide sample answers in German.
**Engagement driver:** Save this + Tag a friend who's also applying
**CTA:** Link to $29 Interview Prep Guide

### Post 5: "I Wish Someone Told Me THIS Before Starting the Approbation Process"
**Format:** Personal story / text post
**Hook:** "I spent $2,000 on documents and translations before I knew this one thing..."
**Content:** Share 3-4 "wisdom bombs" about the process:

- Some states accept digital submissions, others don't
