### Long (LinkedIn About section)

**About me:**
I'm the face behind GermanMedPath, a resource hub for international medical graduates targeting the German healthcare system.

I came into this space because I saw how fragmented the information is for non-EU doctors trying to navigate German Approbation. Government websites are dense. Forums are outdated. Facebook groups are full of conflicting advice.

So I started building what I wished existed: clear timelines, specific document checklists per country, exam prep guides, and honest salary expectations for IMGs in Germany.

Everything I create is reviewed by Dr. Hossam Hassan (MBBS) to make sure the medical content is accurate. I handle the German healthcare system knowledge and pathway mapping.

What I cover:
- Approbation application process (step by step)
- Goethe exam strategy (B2 vs C1, which to target)
- Facharzt specialty comparisons for IMGs
- Fachsprachprüfung prep resources
- State-by-state breakdown of Germany for IMGs
- Salary, contract, and lifestyle expectations

The resource library is free to access. Premium study aids (interview prep, checklists, flashcards) are available for those who want structured guidance.

Non-EU doctor looking at Germany? Start with the free Approbation roadmap. Link in profile.
