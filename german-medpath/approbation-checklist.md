# Approbation Checklist

## 22 Essential Documents + State-by-State Guide

---

## How to Use This Checklist

This is not a generic list. Every document listed here has been verified against the actual requirements of German state medical chambers (Landesärztekammern). Variations by state are noted where applicable.

Print this checklist. Tick off each item as you complete it. The goal is to have all 22 documents ready before you submit your application.

---

## Section 1: Identity & Personal Documents

### [ ] 1. Valid Passport
- Must be valid for at least 2 years
- Include certified copy of all pages (not just the photo page)
- Some states require the original for verification

### [ ] 2. Birth Certificate
- International format preferred
- Certified German translation required
- Apostille or chain legalization if from non-EU country

### [ ] 3. Curriculum Vitae (Lebenslauf)
- Tabular format (table layout)
- Include professional photo (biometric style)
- In German language
- No unexplained gaps (explain any gap with "Weiterbildung" or "Reisezeit")
- Include: education, clinical rotations, employment, research, publications, language skills

### [ ] 4. Passport Photos
- Biometric format (35mm x 45mm)
- White background
- Recent (less than 6 months old)
- Quantity: 3-5 copies

---

## Section 2: Medical Qualifications

### [ ] 5. Medical Degree Certificate
- Original or certified copy
- Include all pages and seals
- Must show: university name, degree awarded, date of graduation
- State-by-state note: Bayern requires the original degree for verification

### [ ] 6. Detailed Transcript of Records
- Must include: hours/credits per subject
- Clinical rotation hours must be detailed
- Some states (Hessen, Baden-Württemberg) require subject-by-subject breakdown
- Translation must include all footnotes and legends

### [ ] 7. Internship/Rotation Certificate
- From your final year clinical rotations (Praktisches Jahr equivalent)
- If your home country didn't have a structured final year, get a letter from your medical school explaining the structure

### [ ] 8. Medical License/Registration
- From the medical council of your home country
- Must state: you are licensed to practice medicine independently
- Must include date of initial registration and registration number

### [ ] 9. Certificate of Good Standing
- From your home country's medical council
- Must be less than 3 months old at time of submission
- States that Germany requires: no pending disciplinary proceedings
- Certificates expire after 3 months. Time your application.

### [ ] 10. Specialist Certificate (if applicable)
- If you completed residency/specialty training in your home country
- Include certificate of completion and logbook if available
- Can reduce Kenntnisprüfung requirements in some states

## Section 3: Language Certificates

### [ ] 11. German Language Certificate
- Goethe-Zertifikat B2 or C1 (recommended)
- Telc Deutsch B2/C1
- TestDaF TDN 4
- DSH-2 or higher

**State requirements vary:**

| State | Minimum for Application | Recommended for Success |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Bayern | B2 | C1 |
| Berlin | B2 | C1 |
| NRW | B2 | C1 |
| Hessen | C1 | C1 |
| Baden-Württemberg | B2 | C1 |
| Sachsen | B2 | B2+ |
| Thüringen | B2 | B2+ |
| Hamburg | B2 | C1 |

### [ ] 12. Fachsprachprüfung Certificate
- If already taken, include certificate
- If not, state in cover letter when you plan to take it

---

## Section 4: Employment & Professional Experience

### [ ] 13. Employment Certificates
- From every clinical position held
- Must include: job title, duration (start and end dates), department, description of duties
- Gap rule: any gap over 3 months needs explanation

### [ ] 14. Research Certificates (if applicable)
- Research positions, publications, clinical trials
- Helps demonstrate competence beyond basic clinical work

---

## Section 5: Legal & Additional Documents

### [ ] 15. Police Clearance Certificate
- From your home country
- From any country where you've lived for more than 6 months in the last 5 years
- Must be less than 3 months old

### [ ] 16. Health Certificate
- Can be obtained in Germany from a Betriebsarzt (occupational physician)
- Includes: tuberculosis screening, hepatitis B, work fitness assessment
- Some states accept home country certificates with translation

### [ ] 17. Proof of Health Insurance
- For the application period (can be travel health insurance)
- For employment: German public insurance

### [ ] 18. Marriage Certificate (if applicable)
- If bringing spouse to Germany
- Include family book if available in your country

### [ ] 19. Children's Birth Certificates (if applicable)
- For child benefits (Kindergeld)
- For family reunification visa

### [ ] 20. German Criminal Record Certificate (Führungszeugnis)
- Can be applied for at your local Bürgeramt after registering in Germany
- If applying from abroad: submit certificate from home country instead

### [ ] 21. Declaration of No Pending Legal Proceedings
- Some states require a formal declaration that no criminal or disciplinary proceedings are pending against you
- Model text included in Section 7

### [ ] 22. Application Fee
- Amount: 100-300 EUR depending on state
- Payment: bank transfer (Überweisung) to the Landesärztekammer
- Include proof of transfer with application
