# Top 5 German States for International Doctors

*Where to apply based on processing times, language requirements, and job availability. With the complete comparison table.*

---

## The Strategy

Not all German states are equally welcoming to IMGs. Some process your Approbation in 8 weeks. Others take 6 months. Some have more jobs than applicants. Others are oversaturated.

**The smart strategy:** Apply for your Approbation in a fast-processing state, then work wherever you want. The license is valid nationwide.

---

## #1 Sachsen (Saxony)

**Processing time:** 8-12 weeks (fastest in Germany)
**Language requirement:** B2
**IMG-friendly:** Very high

Sachsen has the most efficient medical chamber (Sächsische Landesärztekammer) for IMG processing. They have a dedicated international office. The demand for doctors is extremely high across all specialties.

**Drawbacks:** Lower salaries than western states (by about 5-10%). Less English spoken in daily life. Need stronger German to function outside the hospital.

**Best for:** IMGs who want fast processing and an easy entry into the German system. Leipzig and Dresden are affordable cities with good quality of life.

---

## #2 Thüringen (Thuringia)

**Processing time:** 8-12 weeks
**Language requirement:** B2
**IMG-friendly:** Very high

Thüringen has the most severe doctor shortage of any western German state. Hospitals here actively recruit IMGs. Some will even help with relocation costs for hard-to-fill positions.

**Drawbacks:** Mostly rural. Limited subspecialty training compared to university hospitals. Few international schools for families.

**Best for:** IMGs who want to start working quickly and don't mind small-city life. Excellent for Allgemeinmedizin and Psychiatrie.

---

## #3 Nordrhein-Westfalen (NRW)

**Processing time:** 8-16 weeks
**Language requirement:** B2
**IMG-friendly:** High

NRW has the highest hospital density in Germany. The medical chamber (Ärztekammer Nordrhein and Westfalen-Lippe) handles more IMG applications than any other state and has standardized processes.

**Drawbacks:** Variable processing depending on which sub-chamber handles your application. More competition for jobs in Cologne and Düsseldorf.

**Best for:** IMGs who want the widest job market. Cities like Cologne, Essen, and Dortmund have large international communities and excellent hospitals.

---

## #4 Berlin

**Processing time:** 12-20 weeks
**Language requirement:** B2
**IMG-friendly:** High

Berlin has the largest IMG community in Germany. The medical chamber processes applications in English and German. The city is very international.

**Drawbacks:** Longest processing times because of volume. Higher cost of living. Finding housing is difficult. Competition for residency positions is higher.

**Best for:** IMGs who prioritize city life and an international environment. Excellent for research-oriented careers (Charité is a world-class institution).

---

## #5 Bayern (Bavaria)

**Processing time:** 12-20 weeks
**Language requirement:** B2 (C1 strongly recommended)
**IMG-friendly:** Moderate

Bavaria has the strongest economy and highest doctor salaries. Hospitals are well-equipped, and training quality is excellent.

**Drawbacks:** Most conservative medical chamber. They scrutinize applications more heavily. Higher language expectations. More competition for positions in Munich.

**Best for:** IMGs with strong German (C1+) and strong clinical credentials who want top-tier training.

## Recommendations by Situation

**Fastest path to working (4-6 months total):**
Sachsen or Thüringen. Apply for Approbation, search for jobs simultaneously, start in a smaller hospital.

**Best long-term career:** 
NRW or Berlin. More training hospitals, better subspecialty exposure, stronger CV.

**Highest earning potential:**
Bayern or Baden-Württemberg. Higher base salaries and more private patients.

**Best for families:**
Sachsen (Leipzig/Dresden) or NRW (Münster/Freiburg areas). Good schools, safe cities, affordable housing.

---

## How to Choose

1. **Check the Landesärztekammer website** of your top 3 states
2. **Call or email their IMG office** — ask current processing times
3. **Search for jobs** in that state on Indeed.de — see how many open positions exist
4. **Consider your language level honestly** — B2 is not enough for most C1-preferring states
