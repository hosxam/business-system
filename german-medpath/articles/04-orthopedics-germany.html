Top 5 German States for International Doctors

Where to apply based on processing times, language requirements, and job availability.

The Strategy

Not all German states are equally welcoming to IMGs. Some process your Approbation in 8 weeks. Others take 6 months. The smart strategy: apply in a fast-processing state, then work wherever you want. The license is valid nationwide.

#1 Sachsen (Saxony)

Processing time: 8-12 weeks (fastest in Germany)
Language: B2 · IMG-friendly: Very high

Sachsen has the most efficient medical chamber for IMG processing. Dedicated international office. Extremely high demand for doctors. Leipzig and Dresden offer affordable living with good quality of life. Lower salaries (about 5-10% below western states). Less English spoken in daily life.

#2 Thüringen (Thuringia)

Processing time: 8-12 weeks · Language: B2 · IMG-friendly: Very high

Most severe doctor shortage of any western German state. Hospitals actively recruit IMGs. Some help with relocation costs. Mostly rural. Limited subspecialty training. Excellent for Allgemeinmedizin and Psychiatrie.

#3 Nordrhein-Westfalen (NRW)

Processing time: 8-16 weeks · Language: B2 · IMG-friendly: High

Highest hospital density in Germany. Standardized processes. Most job openings. More competition in Cologne and Düsseldorf. Large international communities.

#4 Berlin

Processing time: 12-20 weeks · Language: B2 · IMG-friendly: High

Largest IMG community. Very international city. Longer queues. Higher cost of living. Housing is difficult. Excellent for research (Charité).

#5 Bayern (Bavaria)

Processing time: 12-20 weeks · Language: B2 (C1 preferred) · IMG-friendly: Moderate

Strongest economy, highest salaries, excellent training quality. Most conservative medical chamber. Higher language expectations. More competition in Munich.

Recommendations

Fastest path (4-6 months): Sachsen or Thüringen.
Best career: NRW or Berlin. More training hospitals.
Highest pay: Bayern or Baden-Württemberg.
Best for families: Leipzig/Dresden or Münster area.
