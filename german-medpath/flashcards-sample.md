## Study Tips

1. **Daily practice**: 20 new cards per day, review 80 cards per day
2. **Say it out loud**: Pronunciation matters in clinical settings
3. **Context matters**: Learn the example sentences, not just the word
4. **Group by system**: Study one body system per week
5. **Use in conversation**: Practice with a partner or language exchange
