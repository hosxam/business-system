## How to Use

Terms are organized by body system. Each entry includes:
- German term with article (der/die/das)
- English translation
- Clinical example sentence in German
- English translation of sentence

For optimal learning: cover the right column and test yourself. Make a note of terms you get wrong and review them daily.

## How to Study

**Spaced repetition method:**
1. Study one system per day (20-30 cards)
2. Review previous day's cards
3. Write down terms you consistently get wrong
4. Practice saying each term out loud with the correct German article

**Active recall:**
- Cover the English column and say the German term
- Cover the German column and say the English meaning
- Read the clinical sentence and translate it in your head before reading the English version
