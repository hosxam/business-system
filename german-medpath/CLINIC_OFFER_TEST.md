# Clinic Offer Test Plan

_Test 3 different offers with Dubai clinics. Run in sequence, not parallel._

---

## Offer A: Patient Education Article Pack

### Summary
Write 3 patient-facing blog posts for the clinic. Basic SEO. 500 AED total.

### Target clinic type
General practice, pediatrics, OB-GYN, dermatology — clinics that see high patient volume and benefit from patient education content.

### Subject line
"3 free patient education articles for [Clinic Name]"

### First email
```
Subject: 3 free patient education articles for [Clinic Name]

Hi [Name],

I'm a doctor in Dubai writing patient-friendly medical content for clinics.

I'd like to write 3 sample articles for [Clinic Name] — free, no strings attached.

Topics could be: [suggest 2-3 relevant to their specialty]

If you like them, we can discuss more. If not, no problem.

Interested?

Best,
Dr. Hossam Hassan
```

### Follow-up (Day 7)
```
Subject: Quick follow-up — free articles for [Clinic Name]

Hi [Name],

Just checking in on the free article offer for [Clinic Name].

I know you're busy. The offer stands whenever you're ready.

Would 3 articles on [relevant topic] work for you?

Best,
Dr. Hossam Hassan
```

### Why it may work
- Zero risk for the clinic (free samples)
- Demonstrates quality upfront
- Low commitment barrier

### Why it may fail
- Clinic may never read or respond to the samples
- "Free" can feel like a low-quality trap
- Need to write the samples before getting a yes

### Key risk
You invest writing time before knowing if they'll buy. Mitigate by writing one sample, not three.

## Offer B: Website Content Audit

### Summary
Review the clinic's current website content. Identify gaps, weak pages, missing SEO. Deliver a 1-page audit report. Free.

### Target clinic type
Clinics with poor or outdated websites (most clinics in Dubai fall into this category).

### Subject line
"Quick audit of [Clinic Name] website content"

### First email
```
Subject: Quick audit of [Clinic Name] website content

Hi [Name],

I'm a doctor in Dubai who also writes medical content for clinics.

I noticed [Clinic Name] has [observation about their site — e.g., "no blog", "service pages are thin", "no patient education content"].

I can offer a simple 1-page audit of your current content with recommendations for improvement. No charge, no commitment.

Would that be helpful?

Best,
Dr. Hossam Hassan
```

### Follow-up (Day 7)
```
Subject: Website audit offer for [Clinic Name]

Hi [Name],

Following up on the website audit offer. I've done similar audits for clinics in [area] and the findings usually surprise them.

Happy to share a sample if helpful.

Best,
Dr. Hossam Hassan
```

### Why it may work
- Provides immediate value without requiring content writing
- Shows you understand their business
- The audit naturally leads to "would you like me to fix these issues?"

### Why it may fail
- Clinic admin may not care about their website
- Most clinics have someone (often a relative) who "handles the website"
- Audit without a deliverable feels like a sales pitch

---

## Offer C: Sample SEO Page

### Summary
Write one SEO-optimized service page for a specific procedure the clinic offers. Show them real search rankings potential.

### Target clinic type
Clinics with high-ticket or high-volume procedures (hair transplant, laser, aesthetic, dental implants, weight loss surgery). These clinics actively compete for search traffic.

### Subject line
"One SEO page for [Procedure] — free sample"

### First email
```
Subject: One SEO page for [Procedure] — free sample

Hi [Name],

I'm a doctor in Dubai who writes SEO-optimized content for clinics.

[Procedure] is a highly searched term in Dubai. If [Clinic Name] has a dedicated page for it with proper SEO, it can bring consistent patient leads.

I'd like to write one sample SEO page for [Procedure] — free, no strings attached.

If you like it, we can discuss more pages for other procedures you offer.

Interested?

Best,
Dr. Hossam Hassan
```

### Follow-up (Day 7)
```
Subject: SEO page offer for [Procedure]

Hi [Name],

Following up on the free SEO page for [Procedure].

I've written pages like this for other clinics and the results have been [mention any proof, even if anecdotal].

Would a quick chat this week work?

Best,
Dr. Hossam Hassan
```

### Why it may work
- Concrete deliverable (an actual page they can use)
- Clinics competing for cosmetic/procedure patients pay for SEO
- High perceived value

### Why it may fail
- Most clinic admins don't understand SEO
- They may already have an agency handling this
- The sample page requires real writing time

---

## Recommended Order

**Test Offer A first (Patient Education Article Pack).**

Why:
1. Lowest risk for the clinic (free samples)
2. Every clinic needs patient education content
3. No SEO knowledge required on their part
4. Leads naturally to ongoing content work
5. 500 AED for 3 posts is an easy yes if they see quality

If Offer A gets zero replies after 10 clinic contacts, switch to Offer C (SEO page). SEO is harder to sell but has higher per-client value.

---

## Manual Approval Checklist (Before Sending)

- [ ] Clinic has not been contacted before with the same offer
- [ ] Email address is verified (not the one that bounced earlier)
- [ ] Clinic name is filled in [Clinic Name]
- [ ] Relevant procedure/specialty is mentioned
- [ ] UTM link added to sample (if sample exists)
- [ ] Read out loud to check tone (polite but confident)
- [ ] Sent during business hours (9 AM - 12 PM Dubai time)
- [ ] Tracked in GROWTH_DASHBOARD.md (section 6: Outreach Tracker)

---

## How to Measure Each Offer

| Offer | Success signal | Target | Time frame |
|-------|---------------|--------|------------|
| A: Article pack | Reply asking for sample or call | 1 reply per 10 emails | 7 days |
| B: Website audit | Reply accepting audit | 1 reply per 15 emails | 7 days |
| C: SEO page | Reply agreeing to sample | 1 reply per 12 emails | 7 days |

If Offer A gets 3+ replies from 12 contacts, it's working. Scale it.
If Offer A gets 0 replies from 12 contacts, switch to Offer C.
