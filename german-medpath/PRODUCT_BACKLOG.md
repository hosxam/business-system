## 1. Email Capture System

| Impact | Difficulty | Revenue | Timeline |
|--------|-----------|---------|----------|
| 5 | 3 | High | Later |

**What:** Add a simple email subscription form to the site. When someone downloads the free roadmap, ask for their email first.

**Why it matters:** Right now, you have zero way to follow up with people who download the roadmap. An email list is the most direct path to converting free users to paid customers.

**Current blocker:** Ko-fi already handles the free download without email. To add email capture, you'd need to either:
- Change the Ko-fi product to require email (Ko-fi supports this)
- Or build a separate landing page with email form + download link

**Do now or later:** Later. First, prove that anyone downloads the roadmap at all.

## Summary: Build Order

```
Priority 1 (after first sale):   Email capture
Priority 2 (after 5+ downloads): Roadmap improvement
Priority 3 (after 5+ paid sales): Bundles + FAQ
Priority 4 (after consistent sales): Document checklist generator
Priority 5 (after 10+ sales): Everything else
```

**Fundamental truth:** No improvement matters until someone visits the site and downloads the roadmap. Until that happens, 100% of effort should go to distribution, not product.
