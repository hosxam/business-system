## Decision Rules

Apply these when you fill out the review:

### If 0 site visits after 7 days
- Social platforms are not driving traffic
- Focus on Facebook groups with largest audiences
- Stop posting on LinkedIn temporarily (redirect time to Facebook)
- Add the ToolKit Pro banner immediately

### If visits but 0 roadmap downloads
- The Ko-fi link is not visible or not clickable
- Check: is the link in the right place? Is it broken?
- Test: click the link yourself from each platform
- Make the CTA more specific ("Download here" not "check this out")

### If downloads but 0 paid sales
- People see value in free content but not in paid products
- Review KOFI_PAGE_COPY.md — are the paid product descriptions compelling?
- Consider: add a "why upgrade" section at the end of the free roadmap
- Consider: email capture to follow up with free downloaders

### If posts get engagement but no clicks
- The content resonates but the CTA is weak
- Rewrite the CTA: be more specific about what they get
- Add a "clickable" element — a question, a "I'll share more"
- Test placing the link earlier in the post

### If clinic emails get no replies
- The offer is not compelling enough
- Switch to: offer one free written sample sent directly to them
- Switch to: offer a specific topic relevant to that clinic's specialty
- Try a phone call (if phone number is available) instead of email

### If Facebook posts get rejected by admins
- The group has stricter rules than expected
- Read the group rules carefully
- Remove all links from the post body
- Comment the link instead (if the group allows it)
- Post in other groups instead

### If Reddit account remains restricted after 7 days
- Contact Reddit admins (rarely helps)
- Create a new Reddit account with a different email
- Warm up the new account more gradually (1 week of upvotes only)
- Use the existing account for reading/subscribing only

## Monthly Big Picture Check

Every 4 weeks, ask:

1. Are any products selling yet? If not, why?
2. Which platform has the highest ROI (time spent vs results)?
3. Should I focus on one platform and drop the others?
4. Is the offer right, or does it need to change?
5. Am I helping people first, or am I broadcasting?
