### L3: FSP preparation post (use later this week)

```
What I learned about the Fachsprachprüfung:

It's not a language exam. It's a clinical communication exam.

The difference matters. Goethe tests grammar and reading. The FSP tests whether you can take a patient history in German, explain a procedure, and present a case to a colleague.

Three things to practice:
1. Taking a history (Anamnese) in German, step by step
2. Explaining a diagnosis in plain language
3. Presenting a case with the German clinical workflow

If you're preparing for the FSP, focus on speaking out loud. Reading is not enough.
```
