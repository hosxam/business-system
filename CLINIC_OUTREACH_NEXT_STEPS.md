## Problem Diagnosis

The offer "3 doctor-reviewed blog posts for 500 AED" has three issues:

1. **Too vague.** Clinics don't know what they're getting. "Blog posts" sounds generic.
2. **No proof.** No samples, no portfolio, no examples of past work.
3. **Wrong decision-maker.** Most clinics forward marketing emails to the trash. We need to reach someone who needs content.

---

## Offer Comparison

| Offer | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Blog post package (current) | Simple to explain | Feels generic, no differentiation |
| Website audit + recommendations | High perceived value | Takes time to produce per clinic |
| Free sample homepage section | Low risk for them | Higher effort per clinic |
| Google Business Profile optimization | Immediate value | Less relevant to our product |
| Patient education article pack | Directly useful | Similar to current offer |
| "I made a sample page for your clinic" | Shows actual work | Takes 30-60 min per clinic |

---

## Recommended New Offer

**Free Website Content Audit + 1 Sample Article**

Subject line says: "I wrote a sample medical page for [Clinic Name]"

The approach: Find a page on their site that has weak content (e.g., a specialty page with 2 sentences), write a better version, and send it as a sample. No commitment. Just proof.

This works because:
- It shows rather than tells
- It has immediate value (they can use it for free)
- It demonstrates medical accuracy (Hossam is an MBBS)
- It's harder to ignore than a pitch

**"We don't need blog posts"**
"The sample I sent isn't just a blog post. It's a service page section that ranks on Google. Same format, different purpose."

**"Who is Dr. Hossam?"**
"An MBBS doctor based in Dubai. He writes and reviews all our medical content for accuracy. We don't outsource to non-medical writers."

**"500 AED seems cheap"**
"It's an intro rate while we build our clinic portfolio. The rate will go up after we have enough case studies."

**"Send me more info"**
"Happy to. What specific service would you want content for? I can tailor the next sample to that."
