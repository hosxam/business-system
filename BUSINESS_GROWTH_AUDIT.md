## 3. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS: WHY 0 SALES

### Primary cause: Zero traffic
The site has had exactly zero visitors from any external channel. Social accounts are too new or too low-reach. No SEO traffic. No ads. No word of mouth. You can't sell to nobody.

### Secondary cause: No follow-up system
Even the free roadmap has no email requirement. Someone could download it and we'd never know. No retargeting. No abandoned cart. No newsletter.

### Tertiary causes:
- Low trust on new accounts (LinkedIn has 0 followers, Facebook account is hours old)
- Reddit is locked for 3-7 more days
- Products have weak Ko-fi descriptions
- No social proof anywhere
- Articles have no internal links to products
- ToolKit Pro has no bridge to the main site
- No bundle offer
- No urgency mechanism
