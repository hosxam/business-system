## Email 2: Goethe Strategy (Day 2)

**Subject:** B2 or C1? The honest answer for IMGs

Hi [Name],

This is the most common question I get: "Is B2 enough for Approbation?"

Technically yes, the minimum for most German states is B2.

But here's the difference between IMGs who breeze through the process and those who get stuck:

B2 gets your application accepted. C1 gets you through the oral exam with confidence.

The real test is the Fachsprachprüfung (medical German exam). It tests:
- Taking a patient history in German
- Explaining diagnoses
- Discussing treatment plans
- Writing Arztbriefe (discharge summaries)

B2 is often not enough for this. C1 with medical vocabulary prep is the sweet spot.

If you're short on time (need to start residency ASAP), here's the fast track:
1. Get B2 as fast as possible
2. Start dedicated medical German study immediately
3. Take the Fachsprachprüfung 4-6 weeks after B2

If you have 12+ months, take the longer route to C1. Better foundation, less stress later.
